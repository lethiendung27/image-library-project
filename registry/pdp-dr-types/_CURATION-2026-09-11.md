## 3. CONTENT — six findings that belong in the INSTRUCTION file, not in a type

### 3.1 G3 does not hold uniformly, and the pattern is precise

Four sources use a signal colour against its assigned meaning, and three use it correctly.
The split is not random:

| G3 HOLDS | G3 INVERTS |
|---|---|
| **pressure** — gripi-mata red arrows into a cyan cushion | **heat** — glowy-liff red/orange for therapeutic warmth |
| **verdicts** — hydrovia red cross / green tick, G4-correct | **lift** — fosen-ring red arrows for support |
| **states** — moisosat blue working / red full / green tick | **detection** — snapi-stud green = NO TARGET, red = FOUND |

**The market reaches for the colour of the PHENOMENON, not the colour of the judgement.**
snapi-stud states its code in words (img-14) and then contradicts itself on the same
device (img-15, green = full battery), so the inversion is not even internally consistent.

**And a distinction nothing in this repo states:** a product's OWN indicator colours are not
signal marks. glowy-liff names three LED wavelengths in nanometres; clou-diaxi's teal strip,
snapi-stud's ring, fosen-ring's green battery are all product output. Anyone counting G3
usage in this ledger without holding that distinction will miscount four sources badly.

### 3.2 Five honest-substantiation behaviours, found in the wild

A15 proposes a footnote-and-survey shape, drawn from one corpus frame. The drop found five
better models, each in a real page:

1. **the instrument in shot** — capix-mat photographs a tape measure against the mat's edge, substantiating the same 10cm figure its own headline asserts bare two tiles earlier
2. **the declared error band** — gripi-mata: *"Manual Measurement May Result In An Error Of 1-3 Cm / This Will Not Affect Usage / Please Do Not Purchase If You Mind"*
3. **the comparison scale** — moisosat places 32dB between 15dB (leaves) and 50dB (conversation)
4. **the approximation sign** — moisosat's ≈45% and ≈450ml; snapi-stud's ≤38mm depth maxima
5. **the stated limitation** — snapi-stud: *"(Non-live cables may be detected by metal objects or not detected by the detector)"*, inside a feature tile

**A maximum is falsifiable; a point figure on a render is not.** That single sentence
separates snapi-stud's `≤120mm` from glowy-liff's `4.0mm / 4.5mm`, and it is the shape a
substantiation rule should legislate.

The floor of the scale, for contrast: hydrovia's *"Eases muscle fatigue by up to 30%"* —
no source, no n, no timeframe, and "up to" makes any result satisfy it.

### 3.3 The slot-spending habit — 7 of 10 sources

capix-mat 3 colourway cards · clou-diaxi 2 claim stacks · fosen-ring 3 identity composites ·
glowy-liff **8** plug-and-colourway tiles · gripi-mata 2 colourway demos · mozzapx 4 dissolved
context tiles · snapi-stud 2 display variants.

A direct-response gallery **spends a slot rather than compressing a variable**, and that is
the argument for promoting `03-spec-lineup`, whose whole purpose is to put a matrix in one
frame. The honest counter-argument is in the same corpus: mozzapx img-16 establishes SCALE
against a bench and a building, which a quarter-size grid cell cannot. Compressing costs the
scale cue.

### 3.4 Two rules the corpus breaks on purpose

- **G7, context integrity.** gripi-mata composites a bath mat onto cloud forms; moisosat
  stands a mains dehumidifier in a snowdrift. Two sources, two categories, both to
  literalise a claim. G7 refuses a product in a place it could not be.
- **G13, minors.** pawdi-cas img-04 puts a crying infant in a cot in a bedroom — two of
  G13's three rules breached, and G13 calls that configuration a REFUSAL class. **A
  baby-monitor PDP has a gallery slot this library structurally cannot fill.** The same
  page also carries a compliant frame (img-15, living room, attentive expression) and a
  partial one (img-16, sleeping, private room), which is the most useful G13 comparison the
  ledger holds.

### 3.5 Motion in a still — three instances, two sources, no name

Ghosted multi-position subjects (mozzapx img-18 solar-panel angles; pawdi-cas img-09 a man
crossing a monitored zone) and curved motion arcs used as callout leaders (pawdi-cas img-14
pan and tilt). This registry has no device for showing movement in a still frame.

### 3.6 A third mark class — the compatibility bar

pawdi-cas carries Alexa, Google Assistant, MOES, Smart life, WiFi and Tuya on two frames.
This library has refused two mark classes under the trademark question of 2026-08-18 — the
certification seal and the press mark. A compatibility mark is different in kind: it confers
no authority, it states an interoperability fact a buyer can verify by trying it. Nothing in
G16, G14 or the two LAW rows reaches it; G6 bans logos outright and this page carries twelve.
