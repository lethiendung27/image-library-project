- **No dark frame.** All three are daylight. The product is matte black and emits nothing, so a
  dark ground returns it nothing (ADR-136 decision 3): `section-16`'s two worst-textured frames,
  **6.6 and 9.2 against the owner's 27.4**, were both dark studio.

  blue wires, the car's paint, the shirt. **One clear colour each, and they differ across the set**
  (ADR-119): mid-blue, forest-green, a deep red car. No beige, no charcoal, no grey on grey.
- **The product is READABLE, not necessarily the largest** (ADR-136 decision 2): sharp, lit,
  unobstructed, whole at 390 px.
