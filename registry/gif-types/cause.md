# cause

## PURPOSE
The culprit is at work right now. Dust lifting out of a weave, a pad creeping forward
under a body, a cord tightening into a knot, a beam wandering off its track. The product
is not in the frame and is not implied — this loop indicts the world, not the solution.

## TRIGGER
use_when: >
  The section names what causes the reader's problem and the cause is an event rather
  than a condition. Steps 1 and 2 of the ladder, and the opening beat of a listicle
  entry that indicts an object or a habit.
avoid_when: >
  The problem is a held state the reader inspects — a stripped bed, a pile of what was
  already tried, a room that is simply too hot. Recognition is not a transition, and
  animating it turns a photograph into a performance.

## BOUNDARY
Against `proof` — the product's absence IS the test. The moment the product enters the
frame the loop stops arguing cause and starts arguing outcome, and it must be filed as
`proof`.

The suffering execution and the object-only execution are ONE type. A hand slipping and
a weave shedding grains make the same argument — this bad thing happens — and differ
only in subject class, which the library absorbs as an execution named in `varies_on`,
never as a second type. Precedent: page 65's rung-2 route ran `01-pain-scene` object-only
and said so.

## BRIEF
A shot description in plain words. Name who is using the culprit and where, then what the
culprit does at the moment it does it, then what that leaves them with. The product is
absent and not implied.

**Name the force.** A loop of an object moving on its own is not buildable — a pad creeps
forward because a body is on it and the car brakes, and a weave sheds grains because someone
walks on it. This type's PURPOSE says "under a body" for that reason, so the body belongs in
the shot even where the still it accompanies is object-only.
## NEGATIVE
No text or digits (G6). No product, no branded object, no packaging edge. No comic
exaggeration: a cause loop that overplays reads as an advertisement for the problem and
loses the recognition it exists to win. No reverse loop — a knot that unties itself
argues the opposite of the point.
