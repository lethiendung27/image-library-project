# Argument faults — what an image cannot argue

A cross-type catalogue of the ways a render comes back **correct in every slot and wrong in
what it says**. Every entry was paid for with renders, and every one was found by the owner
looking at an image rather than by any rule in the library.

**Read this before writing the first prompt for a type, and again when auditing one.** These
are not model quirks — the adapter holds those. These are faults in the argument, and they
survive any wording, any style and any model.

Each entry names the types it has been observed on and how often. An entry with observations
on two or more types is a general law; an entry seen once is a warning. New entries are
appended as they are earned; nothing here is deleted, because a fault that stops recurring is
still the reason a rule exists.

---

## A1 · A single-state frame is the product working NOW

A frame that shows the product in use says, of everything in it, "this is what the product is
doing". A harm mark placed there reads as harm the product CAUSES.

- `03-mechanism-ghostbody`: red on both shoulders under an orthopaedic pillow read as the
  pillow hurting the shoulders. Red on the big-toe joint inside a wide shoe read as the shoe
  hurting the toe. 2 of 2 founding renders.
- `02-symptom-rail`: a vapour plume drawn arriving at a face, beside a rail showing a raw red
  nostril, read as the diffuser causing the irritation. 1 render.

**The fix is a place, not a wording.** The wrong state needs somewhere to live — a second
panel, an inset, or a different moment. Where a type has no such place, harm cannot be shown
at all.

## A2 · A mark states; it cannot suppose

A mark can say IS. It cannot say WOULD HAVE, WOULD OTHERWISE, or IS NOT.

- `03-mechanism-ghostbody`: a prompt asked for red "where a narrow shoe would press". The
  render put red on the toe of the shoe being sold. 1 render.
- `02-symptom-rail`: an air purifier's benefit is clean air, which is an absence. Drawing the
  dust it removes drew the problem instead, and the room looked dirty. 1 render.

Corollary: **a harm the product PREVENTS cannot be marked in the frame where the product is
working.** It is the same error as marking something the product does not do.

## A3 · The effect must be locatable

Point at where the product's effect arrives. If you can — a shoulder under a heated wrap, a
hip sunk into foam, a calf under compression — a mark occupies that place and the image needs
nothing from the viewer. If you cannot, no wording rescues it.

- `02-symptom-rail`: air purifier, humidifier and aroma diffuser failed across three separate
  wordings, each obeying its instructions exactly. An air product changes the medium, which is
  everywhere and nowhere, so the viewer must already know that vapour in a room relieves a dry
  throat. 5 renders.

This is an admission test, not a drawing problem. Such products belong in a mechanism type or
a before/after type, not in one that marks a body.

## A4 · The mark must land where the problem is claimed

If the image counts problems in one place and marks the product's action in another, it states
a problem and stands a product beside it.

- `02-symptom-rail`: warm-white contours wrapped a thigh while the rail counted shoulder, knee
  and calf. 4 renders before the rule, clean after it.

Either the product moves to a counted zone, or the counted zones move to the product.

## A5 · Marks go on the body; the product carries only itself

The product is the thing being sold and its own colours are its identity. A mark placed on it
reads as the product being coloured, and the structure the argument is about ends up carrying
nothing.

- `03-mechanism-ghostbody`: `support` blue landed on a pillow's surface instead of on the
  cervical spine; then an entire shoe rendered blue — a signal colour applied to the product.
  2 of 2 renders.

State it in the prompt every time: the product keeps its reference colours and carries no
signal colour at all.

## A6 · A comparison must be of a switchable state

Take the culprit away. If the harmful state goes with it, the comparison is a mechanism. If it
persists, it is accumulated damage, and the corrected panel claims a repair the product cannot
perform.

- `02-cause-anatomy`: a sonic toothbrush does not restore a receded gum; a silk pillowcase does
  not lay a lifted hair cuticle back down. 2 renders, both caught by the owner on the image.

## A7 · A difference below roughly 2:1 cannot be drawn

A dashed measurement line, or any paired mark, cannot show a 20% difference to a reader who is
scrolling. Below that ratio the argument has to come from a different landmark pair, a
different property, or a different type.

- `02-cause-anatomy`: in 18 renders the measure pair never once carried its own difference
  until the comparison itself was made large. 18 renders.

## A8 · A change of state needs a boundary, not two states side by side

Two containers, two tanks or two swatches in one frame are two objects; nothing says one
becomes the other. Put the product ON the boundary of one continuous surface or flow — done
behind it, not-yet-done ahead.

- `02-symptom-rail`: a vacuum's cleaned strip behind the head passed; the same product class
  with two tanks side by side failed; a filtered shower head failed three times because a spray
  has no body and no boundary. 6 renders.

Corollary: **never attach the dulled state to what the person receives.** Unclear water in
cupped hands, in a shower, reads as bathing in dirty water however the prompt is worded.

## A9 · The frame must show a person using a recognisable product

Crop tightly enough and the argument leaves with the body. A cross-section of tissue with an
anonymous shape entering from the edge sells nothing.

- `02-cause-anatomy`: two frame values that cropped to the contact were withdrawn at 0 of 4 —
  a hand and a bicycle gone, a mouthpiece reduced to a sliver. In the same batch 4 of 6 renders
  drew the product as an unnamed grey shape. 10 renders.

## A10 · Direction words belong to the structure, not to the frame

Up, down, across and beneath describe the frame, and the model is free to rotate the frame.
Perpendicular, along, and between describe the structure and hold in any orientation.

- `02-cause-anatomy`: "straight down" in a side cutaway of an eye, where the gap is horizontal,
  produced two renders measuring the wrong axis. 2 renders.
