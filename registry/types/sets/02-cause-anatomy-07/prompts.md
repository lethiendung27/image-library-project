# `02-cause-anatomy` set 7 — six products that change a position, past the admission gate

Written against **`02-cause-anatomy` v1.18**. Owner, 2026-09-15, agreeing to the proposal after set 6:
the RFID wallet from the product file plus five products outside it whose benefit is a change of
position, each with the same culprit, feature and benefit table.

## The gate each product passed

**Position, not effort** (v1.18 SLOT CONSTRAINTS): the product must change a position the body is in —
a posture, an angle, a curve, where weight rests — and it must read with every mark covered. Set 6's
hose clamp plier and steam cleaner sold less force with the hand held the same way, and could not.

| # | product | source | the culprit | the feature, named as a contact | the benefit — the position it changes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RFID blocking bifold wallet | brief 23 | *sitting on it in a back pocket is framed as a posture and back problem* | *tapered bifold shape designed for front-pocket carry* | *keeps the wallet out of a back pocket and off the spine when seated* — the pelvis sits level |
| 2 | upright walker | category, no brief | a short cane with its handle at hip height | forearm supports at chest height | the body stands upright instead of hunching over the cane |
| 3 | height-adjustable ironing board | category, no brief | a low table used for ironing | a board raised to hip height | the spine stays straight instead of bending over the work |
| 4 | elevated dog bowl stand | category, no brief | a bowl on the floor | a stand lifting the bowl to chest height | the dog's neck stays near level instead of bending steeply down |
| 5 | garden kneeler seat | category, no brief | kneeling on bare ground | the kneeler turned over as a low seat | the figure sits upright instead of kneeling bent over |
| 6 | long-handled back scrubber | category, no brief | a hand sponge that must reach the back itself | a long handle over the shoulder | the arm stays in front instead of twisting up behind the back |

**Only the wallet has a brief**, so only its lines are quoted, and a script checks every quote against
the brief file. The other five claim their category's own mechanism and nothing more: no health,
digestion or injury claim for the dog bowl, no treatment claim for any of them. The wallet's brief
bans *minimalist*, *sleek* and *cuts down on pocket bulk*; none reaches a prompt.

## The comparison in each frame

| # | the question the mark answers | left answer | right answer | mark | badge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | where does the pelvis rest? | one sitting bone, raised on the wallet | both sitting bones, level | `pressure` | glyph |
| 2 | where does the upper body rest? | one palm on the cane | both forearms on the supports | `pressure` | thumb |
| 3 | where does the upper body's weight go? | out over the table, onto the lower back | straight down the spine | `load` | hazard |
| 4 | which way does the dog's neck bend? | steeply down | nearly level | `contour` on the neck | none |
| 5 | where does the body's weight rest? | on the kneecaps | on the seat | `pressure` | glyph |
| 6 | which way does the arm open from the shoulder? | behind the back | in front of the chest | `range` | hazard |

**Every change of state is one of kind.** The contours stay off the lower back, where they floated
2 of 2, and the one contour is on a neck.

## Colour, from each product's world

Deep and muted, never the product's own colour, and **no hue name that is light by nature** — set 6's
*sage* came back pale under *deep*. **Nothing stands behind the subject**: no window, wall or hedge,
since set 6 lost its ground to a white wall and a hedge band.

| # | ground | why | style |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | deep muted teal | an office stool; clear of a black or brown wallet | airbrushed — the only style whose figure may wear trousers, which the wallet needs |
| 2 | deep muted warm taupe | a home hallway | line-engraving |
| 3 | deep muted aubergine | a laundry room at evening | ghost-mannequin |
| 4 | deep muted olive | a kitchen floor; clear of a tan dog | flat-vector |
| 5 | deep muted forest green | a garden bed | ghost-mannequin |
| 6 | deep muted spruce | a bathroom's dark tiles | flat-vector |

### Predictions, written before the render

1. **Partial**: the wallet must show in the front pocket through a translucent figure seen from behind.
   Watch whether the model draws it there at all.
2. **Pass**: a palm on a cane against forearms on supports changes posture by kind.
3. **Pass**: bent over a low table against upright at a raised board.
4. **Pass**: the neck line is anchored on the vertebrae, where it has held.
5. **Pass**: kneeling against sitting, the pressure class that has worked.
6. **Partial**: the arm behind the back is the hardest pose in the set.

**Attachments: one reference photo per prompt**; where a product comes in colourways, attach one that is
not red, orange, blue or green. Ratio at the generation tool.
