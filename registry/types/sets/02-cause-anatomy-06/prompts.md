## Where every comparison comes from

Each prompt is built from its brief in `Product Input 8 Sep/` before a word of prompt is written:
**the culprit is the alternative the brief positions against, and the feature and the benefit are the
brief's own lines.** None of the six has a record under this type.

| # | product | brief | the culprit, from its competitor context | the feature, from its feature lines | the benefit |

| 1 | pedal resistance band | 27 | *unassisted sit-ups and crunches ... the neck and hip flexors take over* | *foot stirrups and hand grips joined by elastic tubing, so the band ... assists a movement on the way up* | *a sit-up ... completed with correct form* |

## RESULT — 2026-09-15, five renders, 0 pass

Five renders in `image-library-assets/feedback/` at 14:44–14:45, each mapped to its prompt by content;
**prompt 6, the steam cleaner, has none.** Owner, same day: *"không phải sản phẩm nào cũng có thể dùng
type ảnh này: instant-heat steam cleaner và hose clamp plier chưa thể hiện được"* — not every product can
use this type; those two cannot show their argument in it.

| # | product | verdict | what broke |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | pedal band | partial | the whole spine drawn; red trim on the product; *sage* came back pale |
| 2 | fuel pump | partial | two arrows of each colour where one was asked; blue hoses on the product |
| 3 | window robot | partial | the wall came back white, the named ground only on the floor, so G4 is flat |
| 4 | weed burner | partial | cream engraving paper for a ground; a green hose — **the strongest argument of the set** |
| 5 | hose clamp plier | **fail** | **the product cannot argue in this type**; the rival came back as the reference plier |
| 6 | steam cleaner | — | **not rendered; the owner ruled it out on the same reading** |

**Why the plier and the steamer cannot work here — and it is a class, not two products.** Both sell
*less force*: a ratchet lock that holds a clamp open, a lock-on button that holds a trigger. The hand
grips the tool in the same position either way, so the body has no change of state to draw and the
lock is a small part with nothing to point at. The ledger holds the same class before them: the car
wash spray gun's trigger lock failed, the cord tool failed, the wide-grip pen drew no visible benefit.
**Hand products that change a POSITION rendered** — the vertical mouse's crossed forearm bones, the toe
spacer's angle. **The admission gate is position, not effort.**

**The brief-built comparisons worked where the gate allows them**: the band, the pump, the robot and the
burner all read with the marks covered, each drawing the feature its brief names, and the burner's
flame came back small and blue rather than orange.

What moved into the type is in its 1.18 CHANGELOG.
