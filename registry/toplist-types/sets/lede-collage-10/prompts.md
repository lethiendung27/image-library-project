# `lede-collage` set 10 — the margin becomes a drawn object, and `outlined-panels` gets equal panels

Written against **`lede-collage` v0.19**. Owner, 2026-09-10: *"constraints là ít cần thiết mà
dựa vào chủ yếu do model gen ảnh"*, and *"tôi nghĩ kiểu layout này sẽ không work ... phải là
equal panels"*.

## The record, sorted — and it is not that constraints do not matter

| a clause that names… | outcome across sets 7–9 |
|---|---|
| a **share of the frame** | count **15 of 15** |
| a **shape** — a four-cornered rectangle, half again as tall as wide | `plaque` **2 of 2**, after 3 failures by name |
| a **thing to draw** — cork, pins, curled prints, the same guitar twice | **3 of 3** first-time layouts |
| a **string per type level** | **8 of 8**, after two badges printed their own instructions |
| a **hue relationship** | 3 of 4, and the gradient 1 of 1 |
| a **distance from an edge** | **0 of 12**, four wordings |
| a **prohibition** | 0 of 12, and the badge in a narrow panel 0 of 3 |
| the **register** | not a clause — one prompt gave both, four minutes apart |

**Everything the model must DRAW lands. Everything it must MEASURE does not**, because measuring
is not part of making the picture. A prohibition asks for a measurement with no picture
attached, which is why *"nothing comes within a tenth of any edge"* has never once held.

**So this set stops asking for a gap and draws one.** Cells 1–5 put the whole composition on a
**plate** — a rectangle filling the middle four fifths of the frame, with the ground showing
around it as a plain border. Nothing has to compute a clearance; the clearance is an object with
an edge, and everything lives inside it.

## `outlined-panels` takes EQUAL panels

Owner is right and the corpus said so first. `PARTS/layout` recorded *"the corpus pair are
equal, which is not a requirement"* — the second half was my gloss. Set 9 cell 4 is what it
bought: panels *"of unequal width, stepped to different heights"* returned a **descending
staircase**, and the four non-hero stoves shrank with their panels, so the layout instruction
broke the equal-prominence law from inside and no constraint could have held it.

**Equal was never about all five.** The law says the other FOUR are equal to each other. So the
hero's panel is wider, **the remaining four are equal in width and height and none is stepped**.

## The control

**Cell 6 has no plate** and keeps the blanket *"nothing comes within a tenth of the frame of any
edge"*, on the same layout and badge form as cell 2.

> **Prediction: cell 6's badge and band sit inside G10's 8% band, as they have twelve times.**
> If cell 6 comes back clean the plate bought nothing. If cells 1–5 are clean and cell 6 is not,
> the margin is solved by drawing it.
