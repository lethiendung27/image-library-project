# `lede-collage` set 1 — one layout per cell, and everything `lede-authority` learned

Written against **`lede-collage` v0.7**. Owner instruction, 2026-09-09: *"cần đa dạng layout
hơn, thông thường top list sẽ so sánh 5 sản phẩm, mở style về background tươi sáng, sặc sỡ,
có thể dùng đa dạng nền gradient/solid/… phù hợp để làm nổi bật sản phẩm, visual effect như
half tone, grain, textures"*.

## The curation had to come first, and it moved every number

0.5 recorded that this type held **one** observation of what it defines and five of two other
shapes, and left it. Measuring a clause on that set is the ADR-072/073 failure repeated, so
the pass was taken before anything was written: **nine ledger corrections driven by the
owner's own named example frames**, `proposed lede-mosaic` absorbed and closed at zero, and
the type from 1 match to **7**.

Two things followed immediately:

- **The old ground clause is refuted by its own corrected data.** 0.4 wrote *"either a two-hue
  diagonal gradient or one flat tone, and nothing in between"* — the corrected seven measure
  hue spread at **1°, 2°, 27°, 47°, 98°, 174°, 177°**, continuously. A gradient is a choice
  with a free amount of travel.
- **The badge lost all its evidence.** Both badge-bearing frames were re-filed to
  `lede-winner`. Not one of the seven frames this type now holds carries a mark. The badge is
  permitted by ADR-071 and unevidenced, so **no cell below asks for one** — a frame that wants
  a mark is probably a `lede-winner`.

## What is imported rather than rediscovered

`lede-authority` spent seven sets and 35 renders on problems this type shares. Three findings
carry over as settled, with their evidence:

- **A clause naming two properties that trade against each other must name BOTH.** Proved
  against a control: nine prompts naming chroma alone returned value 0.35 / saturation 0.99;
  three naming value alone returned 0.92 / 0.29; four naming both and refusing both failure
  modes returned 0.93–1.00 / 0.59–0.64. Every ground clause below names both.
- **A stated safe-area percentage does not control LARGE baked type.** G10's 8% was breached
  on 2 of 2 text-bearing renders — round 2 at 4.2% with a self-contradicting prompt, round 3
  at 3.8% with a clean one. So the title is given a **container** to sit inside rather than a
  percentage to respect, which is geometry rather than a number to obey.
- **Invented lettering arrives unbidden.** Five instances across the namespace — a marker pen,
  two collage units, an air fryer, a technician's polo — none of them asked for a brand. Every
  cell below states it.

## The cells — one layout each

| cell | products | layout | ground | tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 travel adapters | `colour-cells` | flat cells, light + coloured | the layout with the most market support |
| 2 | 5 water filter jugs | `open` | one gradient, free travel | the founding layout, and the refuted bimodal claim |
| 3 | 5 sunglasses | `outlined-panels` | one flat ground + thin rules | 2 observations, and the only layout with more than one |
| 4 | 5 desk fans | `rounded-cells` | unequal cells, halftone | the owner's visual-effect ask |
| 5 | 5 leather wallets | `pinboard` | a real corkboard | texture 16.0 — the layout that breaks the smooth-ground clause |
| 6 | 5 kettles | `blocks` + **TITLE** | overlapping blocks | **the only cell with baked type**, and the container test |

**Cell 6 is the one that can fail on G10** and it is deliberately the only one carrying a
title, so a breach is attributable. The other five carry no words at all, which is what six of
the seven corpus frames do.

Five products per cell, thirty in total, none used in any earlier set or round. Five
attachments each — all six render elsewhere (ADR-076). `ELEMENTS` informative, `CONSTRAINTS`
binding; every cell read across both blocks before shipping.

## Coherence test

| cell | correct usage context? | what is the layout arguing? | what is the ground doing? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | presented as an index — cut-outs, no scene | each product owns a cell, so none is favoured | colour separates the cells and lifts small dark plugs |
| 2 | presented on one open field | the five read as one set rather than six compartments | a gradient gives depth a flat tone cannot |
| 3 | presented in equal panels | equal panels are the fairest statement of a shortlist | one ground, so the rules do the dividing |
| 4 | presented in unequal cells | size difference implies a ranking the page owns | halftone gives print texture without breaking smoothness |
| 5 | pinned prints on a board | a board says somebody assembled this by hand | the board IS the ground, and it is photographed |
| 6 | laid across colour blocks | blocks let units overlap and interleave | the title sits in a block of its own |

## 1 — five travel adapters · `colour-cells`

```
TYPE: lede-collage v0.7 — SET 1 CELL 1
REGISTER: graphic product composition, one frame, no words in it.

[PRODUCT REFERENCES] five attached photos, one per unit, in the order given. Each is the
            exact reference for that unit — preserve shape, proportions, material, finish,
            colour and every printed mark exactly. Five different makers.

ELEMENTS — assemble and style these as the picture needs.
[CONDITION] every adapter is clean and as-new — pins bright, casings unscuffed.
[LAYOUT]    the frame divided into five rectangular cells of flat colour, tiled edge to
            edge with no gaps and no gutters. One adapter per cell.
[CUT-OUTS]  each adapter cleanly cut out, no scene behind it, scaled to fill its cell.
[PALETTE]   five colours across the cells, each one BOTH light AND strongly coloured at
            once — a vivid lemon, a bright coral, a clear sky blue at full strength. NOT
            pastels, tints or washed tones; NOT deep, dark or muted ones.
[SHADOW]    a faint contact shadow beneath each unit, identical for all five.

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· Every adapter reads clearly against the cell it stands on.
· No unit favoured by scale relative to its own cell, and no cell larger than another.
· No word, number, badge, mark, rank, price or logo added anywhere in the frame. The
  units carry only the printing their own references carry — invent no lettering.
· No person, no scene, no room.
· Every unit stays clear of every frame edge and of its own cell's border.
```

```

[CONDITION] every jug is clean and as-new — bodies unscratched, lids seated.

            ground in a reading order the eye can follow left to right.
[CUT-OUTS]  each jug cleanly cut out, no scene behind it, all five at the same scale.
[GROUND]    one smooth gradient filling the frame, travelling between two hues. Choose how

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· The gradient is perfectly smooth — no banding, no grain, no vignette, no texture.
· Every jug reads clearly against the part of the gradient it stands on.
· No unit favoured by size, height, position or lighting.
· No word, number, badge, mark, rank, price or logo added anywhere in the frame. The
  units carry only the printing their own references carry — invent no lettering.
· No person, no scene, no room.
```

```
            with even margins between them. One fan per cell.

· Every fan reads clearly against its own cell, including the small cells.
· A cell may be larger than another; no unit is favoured by lighting or by position.
· The halftone never falls across a product, only across ground and cells.
· No word, number, badge, mark, rank, price or logo added anywhere in the frame. The
  units carry only the printing their own references carry — invent no lettering.
· No person, no scene, no room.
```

## 5 — five leather wallets · `pinboard`

**This is the layout that breaks the smooth-ground clause** — the corpus frame measures
texture 16.0 against 1.0–2.8 for the other six, because a corkboard is photographed and the
units are prints rather than knockouts. It is the one cell here whose ground is real.

```
TYPE: lede-collage v0.7 — SET 1 CELL 5
REGISTER: graphic product composition, one frame, no words in it.

[PRODUCT REFERENCES] five attached photos, one per wallet, in the order given. Each is the
            exact reference for that unit — preserve shape, proportions, material, finish,
            colour and every printed mark exactly. Five different makers.

ELEMENTS — assemble and style these as the picture needs.
[CONDITION] every wallet is clean and as-new — leather unmarked, stitching even.
[LAYOUT]    five photographic PRINTS pinned to a corkboard, each print a white-bordered
            rectangle holding one wallet. Prints at slightly different angles and sizes,
            overlapping at the corners in places, each held by a visible pin.
[PRINTS]    inside each print the wallet is photographed square on a plain pale surface,
            filling its print.
[GROUND]    a real corkboard, evenly lit, its grain and edge visible. This ground is
            photographed rather than designed and that is the point of this layout.
[LIGHT]     one soft even light across the whole board; each print casts a small real
            shadow onto the cork.

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· Every wallet reads clearly inside its own print.
· No print favoured by size or position enough to read as a ranking.
· No word, number, badge, mark, rank, price or logo added anywhere in the frame — no
  handwriting on the prints, no labels, no notes pinned beside them. The units carry only
  the printing their own references carry — invent no lettering.
· No person, no scene beyond the board itself.
· Every print stays clear of every frame edge.
```

## 6 — five kettles · `blocks` + TITLE, the container test

**The only cell in this set carrying baked type.** G10's 8% has been breached on 2 of 2
text-bearing renders, including one whose prompt was internally consistent. So the title is
given a CONTAINER to sit inside rather than a percentage to respect.

```
TYPE: lede-collage v0.7 — SET 1 CELL 6
REGISTER: graphic product composition, one frame.

[PRODUCT REFERENCES] five attached photos, one per kettle, in the order given. Each is the
            exact reference for that unit — preserve shape, proportions, material, finish,
            colour and every printed mark exactly. Five different makers.

ELEMENTS — assemble and style these as the picture needs.
[CONDITION] every kettle is clean and as-new — bodies unmarked, spouts bright.
[LAYOUT]    irregular blocks of flat colour overlapping across the frame, the five kettles
            laid across them so units and blocks interleave rather than sit in boxes.
[CUT-OUTS]  each kettle cleanly cut out, all five at a similar scale.
[PALETTE]   the blocks are BOTH light AND strongly coloured at once — NOT pastels, tints or
            washed tones; NOT deep, dark or muted ones.
[TITLE]     one block, upper left, given over to type instead of a kettle: THE BEST FIVE /
            KETTLES, two lines, flat white, left-aligned, set inside that block with a
            clear margin of ground on all four sides of the type.
[SHADOW]    a faint contact shadow beneath each kettle, identical for all five.

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· Every letter of the title sits INSIDE its own block, with visible ground between the
  type and every edge of that block. The block itself does not touch a frame edge.
· Every kettle reads clearly against the blocks behind it.
· No unit favoured by size, height or lighting, and no kettle overlapped by type.
· No badge, mark, rank number, price or logo added anywhere in the frame. The units carry
  only the printing their own references carry — invent no lettering.
· No person, no scene, no room.
· Every unit stays clear of every frame edge.
```

## Grading

| # | question | what it changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Do all six layouts read as the same TYPE?** | `PARTS/layout`. If they do not, the axis is really two types and the absorption ladder has to be run |
| 2 | **Cell 6 — did the title stay inside its block?** | a CONTAINER against a percentage. G10's 8% has failed 2 of 2 on large type; if a container holds, that is the mechanism for every text-bearing type in the namespace |
| 3 | **Did any ground land pastel or dark?** | the paired clause, imported from `lede-authority` with a control behind it. Four of six cells state it |
| 4 | **Cell 2 — how far did the gradient travel, and did it choose?** | the refuted bimodal claim. A free amount of travel is the new reading and it has no render behind it |
| 5 | **Cell 4 — did the halftone stay off the products?** | round 3 got a halftone that behaved; this asks for it on a harder layout |
| 6 | **Cell 5 — does a photographed board still read as this type?** | `pinboard` is one observation and the only layout whose ground is real |
| 7 | **Did any unit come back with invented lettering?** | five instances across the namespace, none of them asked for. Every cell states it; this is the first set to state it in all six |
| 8 | **Did five distinct real units survive in every cell?** | thirty attachments across six frames is the largest multi-reference test the library has run |
