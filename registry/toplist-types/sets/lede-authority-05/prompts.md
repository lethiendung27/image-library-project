# `lede-authority` set 5 — the grid: five product cells and one author cell

Written against **`lede-authority` v0.7**. Owner instruction, 2026-09-09: *"ảnh giày chưa
được vì không nhìn rõ sản phẩm, có thể không cần nhìn thấy author nhưng cần sự so sánh. top
list luôn viết về 5 sản phẩm. riêng case giày có thể có 6 frames: 5 frames giày và 1 frame
author giơ like hoặc tương tác gì đó."*

## What set 4 settled, and the one thing it broke

`field` came back **3 of 3** — the five ranged across one plane with the author beside them,
units legible, brand marks holding beside a human subject. The anchor cell passed, so the
batch was sound.

**Cell 3 failed, and the diagnosis is a framing law rather than a bad shape.** The three
panels held the SAME woman across all three, which is the hardest thing this library asks of
a single pass — and the boots still could not be read, because each panel was framed to the
PERSON. A full-length figure in a cell whose subject is a boot spends the cell on trousers.

**The owner's fix is structural and it is what this set tests:** five cells for five
products, and the author gets **one cell of his own** instead of the whole frame. Every
product is then framed to itself at the largest size its cell can hold, and a person still
stands behind the comparison.

**Five is the count.** Every cell below carries five products, because a top list carries
five.

## The cells

| cell | products | form | author |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 trail running shoes | grid, 5 product cells + 1 author cell | **thumbs-up portrait** |
| 2 | 5 winter jackets | grid, worn — the body IS the product | author cell |
| 3 | 5 espresso machines | grid, objects only, no body needed | author cell |
| 4 | 5 running watches | **FIELD** — the proven form | beside the field |
| 5 | 5 stand mixers | grid, the author cell shows him USING one | author cell, action |
| 6 | 5 backpacks | grid, **six product cells, NO author** | **CONTROL — none** |

**Cell 4 is the anchor.** `field` at 3 of 3 is the form that works; it runs here on a small
wrist-worn product so the five new grid frames have a known-good frame from the same batch
to be judged against.

**Cell 6 is the control and it decides a boundary.** The owner said the author may not need
to be visible. If a six-cell grid with no person reads as well as cell 1, then this shape
belongs to **`lede-collage`**, not to `lede-authority`, and the author cell is decoration.
If it reads as weaker, the author cell is the type. **That is the sharpest question this type
has left**, and one render will not settle it — but one render will say which way to look.

**Cell 2 tests the framing law from the other side.** For a jacket the torso IS the product,
so a full-length or half-length figure per cell is the correct framing — the same composition
that was wrong for a boot. If cell 1 works and cell 2 works, the law is *frame to the
product*, not *never show a person in a cell*.

Six categories, thirty products, none used in sets 1–4 or rounds 1–3. Cells 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5
attach five photos; cell 6 attaches six. All exceed what the owner's app takes, so they ship
in full and render elsewhere (ADR-076). No text layer.

## Coherence test, run before shipping

| cell | correct usage context? | visibly an expert? | what is the background doing? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | each cell is the shoe on a foot mid-stride, which is where a trail shoe lives | the author cell: running kit, and he has clearly just been out in them | one designed ground unifies six cells into one index |

## 1 — five trail running shoes · grid, 5 product cells + author cell

```
TYPE: lede-authority v0.7 — SET 5 CELL 1
REGISTER: editorial grid composition, six cells in one frame, no words in it.

[PRODUCT REFERENCES] five attached photos, one per shoe, in the order given. Each is the
            exact reference for that shoe — preserve shape, proportions, material, finish,
            colour and every printed mark exactly. Five different makers.

ELEMENTS — assemble and style these as the picture needs.
[CONDITION] every shoe is clean and as-new — uppers unmarked, outsoles unworn.
[GRID]      six equal rectangular cells in one frame, tiled edge to edge, each outlined
            by a thin rule against one shared designed ground.
[PRODUCT CELLS] five cells, one shoe each in the order of the attachments. Each cell is
            framed to the SHOE: cropped at mid-calf, the foot filling most of its cell,
            the shoe turned so its upper, midsole and outsole edge read.
[AUTHOR CELL] the sixth cell: a man in his thirties in trail running kit, cropped at the
            chest, giving a plain thumbs-up to the lens, composed and unhurried.
[GROUND]    one flat strongly saturated colour shared by all six cells.
[LIGHT]     one identical soft key in every cell.
[GRADE]     a clean natural palette, skin held true.

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· Every cell is framed to its own subject. No cell shows a full-length figure.
· All six cells are the same size and share the same ground, light and outline.
· One person only, in the author cell alone. No person in any product cell above the calf.
· No name, caption, title card, lanyard, certificate or any word or number in the frame.
· Every shoe is clean, undamaged, and clear of its cell's outline.
```

## 4 — five running watches · FIELD, the anchor

**This cell is expected to PASS.** It is the form set 4 landed three times out of three, run
here on a small wrist-worn product so the grid cells have a known-good frame from the same
batch to be graded against.

```
TYPE: lede-authority v0.7 — SET 5 CELL 4, ANCHOR
REGISTER: editorial portrait photograph, one frame, no words in it.

[PRODUCT REFERENCES] five attached photos, one per watch, in the order given. Each is the
            exact reference for that watch — preserve shape, proportions, material,
            finish, colour and every printed mark exactly. Five different makers.

ELEMENTS — assemble and style these as the picture needs.
[CONDITION] every watch is clean and as-new — cases unscratched, straps unworn.
[FIELD]     four of the five laid in a row across the lower third of the frame on a plane
            at chest height, evenly spaced, each face turned to the lens.
[HERO]      the fifth worn on the subject's wrist, the arm raised so its face reads.
[SUBJECT]   a distance runner in his forties in plain running kit, standing behind the row.
[ADDRESS]   looking into the lens over the raised wrist, composed, speaking evenly.
[GROUND]    a plain studio backdrop in one strong saturated colour, filling the frame
            behind him and the row. No room, no set, no props but the five watches.
[LIGHT]     a soft broad key for the whole group, the backdrop lit evenly.
[GRADE]     a clean natural palette, skin held true, no cast from the backdrop.

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· Editorial register: this photograph is made by somebody else. No selfie framing, no
  arm's-length camera, no phone in shot, no front-camera distortion.
· One person only. No second person anywhere in frame.
· His expression is settled. Not startled, not mid-flinch, not caught off guard.
· The four in the row are equal in size, height and lighting; only the worn one is raised.
· No name, caption, title card, lanyard or certificate, and no word or number that names
  or qualifies a person. No reading is legible on any watch face.
· Every watch stays clear of every frame edge.
```

## Grading

| # | question | what it changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cell 1 — is each shoe legible at cell size?** | the framing law. Set 4's panels failed on exactly this and the fix is structural rather than stylistic |
| 2 | **Cell 6 beside cell 1 — does the author cell add anything?** | the boundary against `lede-collage`. If it does not, this shape is not `lede-authority` and the type keeps only `single` and `field` |
| 3 | **Cell 2 — does a worn product justify a body in its cell?** | whether the law is *frame to the product* or *never a person in a cell* |
| 4 | **Cells 1, 2, 5 — does the author cell read as the page's reviewer, or as a stock model?** | whether one cell is enough to carry standing, now that dress has less room to work |
| 5 | **Cell 5 — can the author cell be an ACTION rather than a portrait?** | whether the sixth cell has to be a face at the lens |
| 6 | **Did five and six distinct real units survive across separate cells?** | the multi-reference route has never been asked to keep units apart in separate frames rather than side by side |
| 7 | **Cell 4 — does the anchor pass?** | if it does not, the batch was a bad day and nothing above is evidence |
| 8 | **Cell 2 — is it the same woman in all six cells?** | same-identity across panels is adapter Rule 5's hardest case; set 4 cell 3 held it across three |
