# `lede-authority` set 1 — six prompts, six products, one variable at a time

Written against **`lede-authority` v0.3**, the version that took the file from `reserved` to
`active` and gave it its first SKELETON. Owner instruction, 2026-09-09: work type by type,
six prompts on six arbitrary products, loop until the output is good.

**This set is not six attempts at the same picture.** The type has exactly one open question
and it is the one that decides whether the type lives: **what makes a person read as an
authority once nothing may be printed?** `PARTS/address` records two candidate answers with
2 and 1 observations behind them. The six cells below isolate them, and two of the six are
deliberately built to FAIL.

| cell | product | address | second person | ground | what it isolates |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ultrasonic jewellery cleaner | `interview` | **yes** | contextual | round 3's unasked finding, tested on purpose — recurrence at 2 |
| 2 | cast-iron skillet | `to-lens` | no | contextual | direct address ALONE, with no interlocutor to help |
| 3 | large-diaphragm studio microphone | `to-lens` | no | **studio** | the owner's own named form, at 1 observation |
| 4 | trail running vest | `to-lens`, presenting | no | contextual | the holding-it-up gesture |
| 5 | cordless orbital sander | **candid** | no | contextual | **CONTROL — built to read as `lede-inuse`** |
| 6 | orthopaedic dog harness | `to-lens`, at rest | no | contextual | **CONTROL — built to read as a portrait of a person** |

**Cells 5 and 6 are controls and are expected to fail as `lede-authority`.** Five candidates
and no counter-example would tell us nothing: if cell 5 reads the same as cell 2, the
boundary against `lede-inuse` is not visible and the type folds. If cell 6 reads as a
portrait, `slot-rules.md`'s `author` row reaches further than v0.3 argues. **A set without a
control cannot fail, and a test that cannot fail is not a test.**

Six products, none used in rounds 1, 2 or 3. One attachment each. No text layer, so G16
does not bind. `ELEMENTS` is informative and `CONSTRAINTS` is binding, per round 3 — where
constraints held 14 of 18 and two of the four breaks were the prompt author contradicting
himself across the two blocks. **Every cell below was read across before shipping.**

## 1 — ultrasonic jewellery cleaner · `interview`, second person in frame

```
TYPE: lede-authority v0.3 — SET 1 CELL 1
REGISTER: editorial documentary photograph, one frame, no words in it.

[PRODUCT REFERENCE] the attached photo is the exact reference for the ultrasonic jewellery
            cleaner. Preserve shape, proportions, material, finish and colour exactly.

ELEMENTS — assemble and style these as the picture needs.
[SUBJECT]   a woman in her fifties at a jeweller's bench in a work apron, the cleaner open
            in front of her, a ring just lifted from its basket between her fingers.
[ADDRESS]   she is answering someone — turned away from the bench toward a second person
            who stands at the frame edge with their back to the lens.
[MANNER]    matter-of-fact, mid-sentence, explaining something she knows well.
[PLACE]     a real working jeweller's bench: pegwood, a loupe, a tray of findings, the
            ordinary clutter of the trade.
[LIGHT]     the bench's own task lamp with daylight from a side window.
[GRADE]     a natural palette, light grain. Honest, not styled.
[GROUND]    the workshop behind her, thrown out of focus, plainly lit.

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· Editorial register: this photograph is made by somebody else. No selfie framing, no
  arm's-length camera, no phone in shot, no front-camera distortion.
· Nothing credentials her: no name, caption, title card, certificate, diploma, framed
  award, lanyard, medal, clipboard, or white coat.
· No printed word or number anywhere that names or qualifies a person.
· Both people are adults, and neither is a recognisable public figure.
· The product stays clear of every frame edge.
```

## Grading

A verdict of `pass`, `partial` or `fail` per render. Then:

| # | question | what it changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cell 5 beside cell 2 — can you tell them apart?** | the only boundary this type has. If not, `lede-authority` folds into `lede-inuse` under an `address` axis and the file goes |
| 2 | **Cell 6 — does it read as a report on a product, or as a portrait of a person?** | whether the type needs an ACT as well as an address, and whether `slot-rules.md`'s `author` row reaches after all |
| 3 | **Cell 1 — does the interlocutor produce the interview register a second time?** | `PARTS/address` `interview` at 1 observation. Two would make it the type's spine |
| 4 | **Cells 2, 3, 4 — which of them reads as somebody worth believing, with nothing printed?** | which `address` form the SKELETON should lead with |
| 5 | **Cell 3 — does a studio backdrop still carry standing, with the place gone?** | `PARTS/ground` `studio` at 1 observation. The type's stated mechanism is that the PLACE credentials; a studio removes it |
| 6 | **Did the editorial-register constraint hold — is any frame a selfie?** | the G14 firewall, which is the one refusal that genuinely binds this type |
| 7 | **Did anything credential a subject unasked — a coat, a lanyard, a certificate on a wall?** | whether the NEGATIVE block is complete or is missing a form the model reaches for |
| 8 | **How many CONSTRAINTS held across the six?** | round 3 ran 14 of 18. Set 1 is the first six-cell set written in this form |
