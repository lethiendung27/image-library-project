# `lede-inuse` set 1 — the crop its own corpus shows, against the crop it copied

Written against **`lede-inuse` v0.4**. First set for this type; its only earlier render is the
founding round of 2026-09-09, `partial`.

## Where this type actually stands

**One render, and it failed on ARGUMENT while every slot landed.** A wall-mounted air cooler
above a bed: the cooler read as the reason the room was bearable rather than as the subject,
which is the hardest thing this type asks for — and the sleeper read as **still overheated**, so
the frame argued the pain this type is the relief half of. The prompt built it, by asking for a
plainly hot room and leaving the release to the product's presence.

**Two corpus observations, and neither looks like the file.**

| | what it is | value | texture |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1920.avif`, **match** | a man brushing his teeth — close on head and hand, dark bathroom, eyes off-lens | **0.16** | 10.0 |
| `WILI_BEDROCK`, variant | feet in sandals on a sunlit rock, **no person above the shin**, inside a designed pink mat on a mint ground | **0.94** | 3.1 |

Both crop **tight to the product ON a body**. The copied `subject` and `environment` describe a
whole person mid-errand in a public place with blurred passers-by, which is `06-relief-scene`'s
shape on a product page and has one render here — the one that failed.

**n=2 is under SPEC §6.2's threshold of three, so nothing above is a rule.** This set tests it.

## The G14 firewall, new at 0.4 and in every cell

`registry/toplist-instruction.md` records G14 binding the SLOT: *a lede image that reads as a
customer's own photograph, beside a ranking the page presents as editorial, is the shape G14
exists to refuse.* The register this file copied — *"a candid documentary photograph a passer-by
could have taken"* — names that register.

`lede-authority` resolved the same problem with a distinction: **the frame is made by a
PHOTOGRAPHER, not by the subject.** Candid stays; amateur goes. Every cell below carries the
visible tells as refusals, and the tight crop is itself part of the answer — a frame with no face
and no camera-holder cannot read as a selfie.

Cells 5 and 6 are the same configuration on different products, which gives the parent's shape
two draws rather than one — `registry/toplist-instruction.md` records that one render is one
draw and that a control needs a repeat.

> **Prediction, before the render: cells 5 and 6 fail** the way the founding round failed — the
> release does not read, or the frame reads as stock photography. **If both come back arguing
> relief, the copied wide scene is fine and the corpus's tight crop is a market habit rather
> than a requirement.**

Six products, none used in any earlier set or round. One reference photo each. All six render
elsewhere (ADR-076).
