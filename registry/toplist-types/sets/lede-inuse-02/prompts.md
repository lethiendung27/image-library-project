# `lede-inuse` set 2 — one method per cell, against set 1's absences

Written against **`lede-inuse` v0.5**. Owner, 2026-09-10: *"sự dễ chịu chưa được thể hiện rõ qua
thái độ (mặt), môi trường (nếu không có mặt)... gợi ý các phương pháp show được lợi ích sản phẩm
đem lại một cách trực tiếp, dễ hiểu, chân thực"*.

## What set 1 settled

**The firewall works, 6 of 6.** No frame read as a customer's own photograph on its first
outing. **The designed mat works, 2 of 2.** Both are kept and neither is varied here.

**The crop hypothesis is refuted.** 0.4 predicted the tight crop would read better than the
parent's wide scene. The wide wrist-brace frame is composed as well as the tight knee-brace one
and both fail on the same thing, so **crop is not the variable**.

**Benefit visible in 1 of 6**, and the one that worked is the headlamp — a beam of real light
falling on rock and on the hands clipping a carabiner. The product's effect was an OBJECT in the
frame.

## The diagnosis, and it is mine

Set 1's six prompts asked for *not guarding*, *no wince*, *no hand braced*, *calm and
unbothered*, *not squinting, not flushed*. **Every one is a prohibition**, and this namespace has
measured prohibitions at **0 of 12** against **37 of 37** for clauses naming a thing to draw. An
absence returns NEUTRAL, and neutral is not ease.

**And I built the problem into two prompts.** The founding round asked for *"a room that is
plainly hot"*; set 1 cell 5 asked for *"the cold is plainly real"*. Both then left the relief to
the product's presence, and both returned the problem. **No cell below describes a symptom.**

> **Prediction: cell 6 returns a neutral frame** — a person wearing a brace, doing a thing, with
> nothing showing the brace works — as five of six did in set 1. **If cell 6 reads as relieved,
> the absences were never the fault and the seven methods are decoration.**

Six products, none used in any earlier set or round. One reference photo each. All six render
elsewhere (ADR-076).
