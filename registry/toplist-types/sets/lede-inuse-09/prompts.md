# `lede-inuse` set 9 — the full surface, at the settled length

Written against **`lede-inuse` v0.14**. Owner, 2026-09-10: *"set 9 đầy đủ"*.

## Why this one is a coverage set

**Set 8 was the first set in this type's history where nothing failed.** Six of six pass, the length
cut vindicated, the physics fix landing 1 of 1, and no regression on any of the six clauses that had
been holding. There is no open fault to build a one-variable experiment around.

**So set 9 puts every case the type declares into one set at the settled length**, and the two cells
set 8 never drew come with it. Thirteen cells.

**Its limit, stated up front as set 6's was:** with this many cases, a failure in one cell cannot be
attributed the way a controlled set attributes it. What it can do is show the whole surface at once
— which is what *đầy đủ* asks for — and give the framed variant the draws it has been missing.

## What set 8 settled

**The cut holds.** Five constraint lines removed from four cells and **0 of 5 faults appeared**: no
badge, no overlay, no retouched skin, no second person, no guarded body, and clothing still
particular because `[DRESS]` names it. The uncut control agreed on every axis. **The floor is ~1974
characters and every cell below is written to it.**

**The physics fix repairs the fault the owner named.** The suede cell asked for *no line at all* —
a nap thinning unevenly into worn leather with strokes showing across it — and returned exactly
that. Cell 4 below is its second draw, on rust rather than suede.

**Objects read LEFT, 6 of 6**, once the prompt named how they got there.

**Four framed cells, which is the point of the set as much as the coverage is.** The variant has
been drawn three times in eight sets and passed 3 of 3, and it fell out of the loop because a mat
interacts with no fault. `lede-inuse.md` 0.13 made it structural. Cells 10 and 11 are set 8's two,
which were written after that render went out and never drew.

**Cells 12 and 13 are the never-drawn case and they are the repeat.** All three framed cells so far
are tight crops with no face in them, so **nobody knows whether a mat can hold a portrait** — whether
the face survives the reduction, and whether a framed face drifts toward `lede-authority`'s register
the way an open pose did. Two identical draws, because that is a register question.

> **Prediction: the mat holds a face.** The reduction costs perhaps a third of the frame and this
> type's faces are already three-quarter and close. **If either draw comes back with the face
> shrunk past reading, or looking out of the mat at the viewer, the framed variant is a NO-FACE form**
> and the type file should say so.

Predictions for the rest, written before the render:

- **The scalloped and the square mat both land**, and the mat's shape is a free choice rather than
  one fixed form.
- **Cell 4's rust boundary is ragged**, like cell 3 of set 8 — bare metal thinning irregularly into
  scale, no straight edge anywhere.
- **Cell 8 is a scene, not a packshot**, as set 6's insole cell was.
- **Nothing regresses**: casting, dress, gaze, ease, light, condition and the lived-in room have
  held for three sets.

Twelve products, none used in any earlier set or round of this namespace. One reference photo each,
one generation call each (ADR-021, ADR-076). Grade on the calibrated instrument — median value,
share of pixels below 0.15, **mean** saturation.

## Prompt economy — written to set 8's measured floor

| | median | range |
|---|---|---|
| `lede-inuse-07` | 2438 | 2040–2491 |
| `lede-inuse-08`, cut cells | 1974 | 1908–2256 |
| **`lede-inuse-09`, all 13** | **2018** | 1865–2317 |
| — of which the 9 plain cells | 1962 | 1865–2061 |
| — of which the 4 framed cells | 2282 | 2140–2317 |

**No draft went over the ceiling, for the first time in four sets.** The five lines set 8 proved to
be ballast are out of all thirteen cells, and that removal is what paid for the framed variant's two
extra blocks. **A framed cell costs about 320 characters** — `[MAT]`, `[GROUND]` and the swapped
refusal — and that is the honest price of the case the owner asked about.

Nothing else was cut. Every clause in these prompts has either failed a render in this type's
history or been confirmed against a control, and the ratchet is broken because a removal finally ran
as an experiment instead of as a hope.

**Three deliberate wording departures**, so a grader does not read them as slips: the no-face cells
carry the ease floor as *in the body or the hands*; cell 9 adds *no phone at a mirror* because it is
the one cell that goes near G14's line; and the four framed cells swap the drawn-element refusal for
*the mat and the ground are the ONLY designed elements*.
