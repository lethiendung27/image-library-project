# `lede-collage` set 12 — graphics narrow to surface treatments, and `split-frame` gets two angles

Written against **`lede-collage` v0.21**. Owner, 2026-09-10, on set 11.

## What set 11 closed

**The white frame.** Naming the border's VALUE instead of its role moved it from near-white to
deep and saturated in 5 of 5, and the plate from 60–85% to 82–93%:

| | set 10 | set 11 |
|---|---|---|
| plate covers | 60–85% | **82–93%** |
| border saturation | 0.02 | **0.67 – 0.97** |
| border value | 0.95 — white | **0.36 – 0.54 — deep** |

**Filled fields 5 of 5**, no pastel cells. **No rank numbers 8 of 8.** **Count 8 of 8**, geometry
now 29 of 29.

## The two owner corrections this set carries

**1 — graphics stop at SURFACE TREATMENTS.** *"graphic element chỉ nên dừng lại ở half tone
effect và gradient, grainy, không tự vẽ thêm gây rối hình"*. This reverses the 2026-09-09
instruction that created `PARTS/graphics`, and it is recorded as a reversal.

**Kept: halftone, gradient, grain** — treatments of a surface already in the picture.
**Retired: everything drawn** — glyph fields, notched keylines, outline echoes, icon pairs,
dotted silhouettes, radiating arcs. Set 11 cell 4's arcs read as a target behind the hero and
cell 1's dotted outlines traced every umbrella rib.

*Two retired elements carry 2 corpus observations each, so this departs from the corpus on
instruction rather than on evidence. The one exception is `split-frame`'s glyph field: it is not
an accent laid over a composition, it IS that layout's designed half, and both corpus frames
carry one.*

**2 — `split-frame` is two ANGLES.** *"tôi muốn cùng sản phẩm nhưng góc chụp khác nhau như tham
chiếu (có thể là sử dụng, có thể là showcase góc khác)"*, pointing at this type's own corpus
frame `FI-COTOPAXI-CORAZA-SUITCASE`: a closed suitcase cut out front-on, beside **the same
suitcase opened flat, seen from above, with packing cubes and a hand pressing one down**.

Both of this type's `split-frame` renders gave the same view twice — a cut-out beside the
identical pose with a background behind it. **`polaroid` gets this right because its prompts
NAME the views**, so cell 7 below names both of its own, and the photographed half shows the
product in use.

## One clause of mine, restored

Set 11's `pinboard` printed five product names under its prints. Set 9 carried *"No words in the
frame but those named above"* and set 11 dropped it — the second earned clause lost in a
rewrite. It is back in all eight cells.
