# `lede-collage` set 2 — several hues, asymmetric cells, and a badge with a design on it

Written against **`lede-collage` v0.8**. Owner verdict on set 1, 2026-09-09: *"màu sắc, bố
cục chưa sáng tạo. chi tiết 'the best five kettles' mới chỉ dừng lại ở chữ trên 1 nền đơn
điệu. hãy thêm badge với style riêng, đặc sắc. ảnh kính layout chưa sáng tạo."*

## Both faults were written by me, and set 1 attributes them exactly

| cell | what my prompt asked for | what came back | owner named it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 sunglasses | *"one flat tone shared by all five panels"* + *"all five panels the same width and the same height"* | one yellow field, five identical panels — a spec sheet | **yes** |
| 4 fans | *"its own lighter or deeper shade of that same hue"* | five cells of near-identical cyan | implied by *"màu sắc"* |
| 1 adapters | five different colours, cells of unequal size | alive | no |
| 6 kettles | overlapping blocks, no single hue named | alive | only its title |

**The two cells the owner named are the two whose prompts asked for monochrome and
uniformity in my own words.** Nothing about the renderer produced this.

### The trap, and this is its third instance in one day

| clause, written against one real fault | what it removed |
|---|---|
| `lede-authority` *"the expression is settled"* | every pose, across twelve renders |
| `lede-authority` *"the backdrop is lighter than the product"* | all chroma, on white units |
| `lede-collage` *"all five panels the same width and height"* | the whole layout axis |

**A constraint aimed at one fault but written as a general property removes the entire
dimension it touches.** Each repair was a finer distinction, never the opposite — deliberate
against caught, separation against lightness, and now **PROMINENCE against uniformity**. The
no-favoured-unit law is about no unit being made to look more important: not larger relative
to its own cell, not brighter, not marked. **It never required the cells to be identical, and
not one corpus frame is a uniform grid.**

## Three changes in every cell

- **SEVERAL hues across the cells.** Different hues, not one hue in five shades. The corpus
  runs four cell colours in `GIFTS-TABLETOP` and three in `LABOR-DAY`.
- **Cells may differ in size, shape and position.** Only prominence is held equal.
- **A BADGE, with a design on it.** The owner asked for one, so it arrives on instruction
  rather than on corpus support — this type's own MARKS table is at zero because both
  badge-bearing frames were re-filed to `lede-winner` in the curation. Each badge below is a
  different form, and every one owes what `lede-winner`'s owes: **at least one internal tone
  step and at least two type sizes.** A flat block with one size of type is what set 1's
  title was and what the owner called *"nền đơn điệu"*.

Set 1's container instruction is kept — the title stayed inside its block on 1 of 1, where a
stated percentage failed 2 of 2 — but the block is now an object rather than a rectangle.

## The cells

| cell | products | layout | badge form | palette |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 electric toothbrushes | `colour-cells`, unequal | `plaque` | 5 hues |
| 2 | 5 hand blenders | `blocks`, overlapping | `rosette` | 4 hues |
| 3 | 5 reading lamps | `outlined-panels`, **unequal widths** | `ribbon` | 3 hues + a gradient |
| 4 | 5 backpacks | `rounded-cells` + grain | `band` | 4 hues |
| 5 | 5 chef knives | `open`, one gradient | `seal` | gradient, 2 hues |
| 6 | 5 headphones | `colour-cells` | **NO badge, NO title — CONTROL** | 5 hues |

**Cell 3 is the direct answer to *"ảnh kính layout chưa sáng tạo"***: the same layout family
as the sunglasses frame, with the uniformity removed — panels of different widths, one
running full height, the others stepped.

**Cell 6 is the control.** It is cell 1's construction with the words and the mark taken away.
If it reads as well as the badged cells, the badge is decoration on this type and the owner's
instruction has a cost worth knowing; if it reads flatter, the badge earns its place. Either
way the type learns something it cannot learn from six badged frames.

Thirty products, none used in set 1 or in any earlier set or round. Five attachments each —
all six render elsewhere (ADR-076).

## 1 — five electric toothbrushes · `colour-cells` unequal · `plaque`

```
TYPE: lede-collage v0.8 — SET 2 CELL 1
REGISTER: graphic product composition, one frame.

[PRODUCT REFERENCES] five attached photos, one per unit, in the order given. Each is the
            exact reference for that unit — preserve shape, proportions, material, finish,
            colour and every printed mark exactly. Five different makers.

ELEMENTS — assemble and style these as the picture needs.
[CONDITION] every brush is clean and as-new — heads unworn, handles unmarked.
[LAYOUT]    five rectangular cells of UNEQUAL size tiled edge to edge with no gaps. One
            brush per cell, each cut out and scaled to fill its own cell.
[PALETTE]   five DIFFERENT hues, one per cell — not one colour in five shades. Every one is
            BOTH light AND strongly coloured at once: NOT pastels, tints or washed tones;
            NOT deep, dark or muted ones.
[BADGE]     form `plaque`: an upright rectangle sitting across the boundary between two
            cells, in a colour none of the five cells carries. Give it an interior — a
            thin rule inset from its edge — and two type sizes: BEST FIVE large over 2026
            small beneath.
[SHADOW]    a faint contact shadow beneath each brush, identical for all five.

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· Cells differ in size; no unit is favoured by scale RELATIVE TO ITS OWN CELL, by lighting,
  or by being the one the badge sits on.
· Every brush reads clearly against the cell it stands on.
· Every letter on the plaque sits inside the plaque with visible margin on all four sides,
  and the plaque does not touch a frame edge.
· No rank number, price, star row, certification seal, press logo or third-party award
  mark. The units carry only the printing their own references carry — invent no lettering.
· No person, no scene, no room.
```

## 6 — five headphones · `colour-cells` · CONTROL, no badge and no title

**This cell carries no words and no mark at all.** It is cell 1's construction with the text
layer removed, and it exists so the badge can be judged against its own absence in the same
batch. Six of the seven corpus frames carry no mark, so this is also the form the corpus
actually supports.

```
TYPE: lede-collage v0.8 — SET 2 CELL 6, CONTROL
REGISTER: graphic product composition, one frame, no words in it.

[PRODUCT REFERENCES] five attached photos, one per unit, in the order given. Each is the

[CONDITION] every pair is clean and as-new — earcups unmarked, headbands unworn.
[LAYOUT]    five rectangular cells of UNEQUAL size tiled edge to edge with no gaps. One
            pair per cell, each cut out and scaled to fill its own cell.
[PALETTE]   five DIFFERENT hues, one per cell — not one colour in five shades. Every one is
            BOTH light AND strongly coloured at once: NOT pastels, tints or washed tones;
            NOT deep, dark or muted ones.
[SHADOW]    a faint contact shadow beneath each pair, identical for all five.

CONSTRAINTS — binding.
· Cells differ in size; no unit is favoured by scale RELATIVE TO ITS OWN CELL or by lighting.
· Every pair reads clearly against the cell it stands on.
· No word, number, badge, mark, rank, price or logo added anywhere in the frame. The units
  carry only the printing their own references carry — invent no lettering.
```
