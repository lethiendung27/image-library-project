# Toplist round 2 — seven prompts, and this is the set to render

**Round 1 is superseded and kept as the record.** It was written before four corrections
that changed what these types are: `lede-winner` rewritten end to end after the owner named
three examples (ADR-074), the ground rules rebuilt from this namespace's own corpus
(ADR-073), the competitor brand-mark clause lifted (ADR-075), and the attachment cap lifted
(ADR-076). Six of the seven types are now `active`; only `lede-authority` is reserved.

**Ratio is not stated anywhere.** The app resolves the lede ratio; ADR-016 and adapter
Rule 4 keep it out of prompt text regardless.

## Attachments, and the thing that changed

| # | type | v | attachments | text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `lede-pain` | 0.2 | **none** — the product is absent by definition | no |
| 2 | `lede-inuse` | 0.2 | 1 | no |
| 3 | `lede-lineup` | 0.4 | **5, one per unit** | no |
| 4 | `lede-testing` | 0.3 | 1 | no |
| 5 | `lede-winner` | 0.5 | 1 | **yes** — display type + mark |
| 6 | `lede-collage` | 0.5 | **4, one per unit** | **yes** — title + mark |
| 7 | `lede-authority` | 0.1 | 1 | no |

**3 and 6 ask for several photos and that is now legal** (ADR-076, owner instruction). One
prompt, one generation call, nothing assembled afterwards — only the COUNT of attachments
changed. Where the owner's own app takes one, **these ship in full and are rendered
elsewhere**; a prompt is not trimmed to fit a tool.

**They may also carry real brands** (ADR-075): `SPEC.md` §6.4's competitor clause does not
bind this namespace, and with a reference attached per unit the products are the real named
ones. What stays refused is a certification seal, a press logo or a third-party AWARD mark
— G16's `LAW, not taste` row, which the corpus breaches in 3 of `lede-winner`'s 5 frames
and which is the owner's next decision.

## Grounds are per family and measured on this corpus

One number splits the namespace with no overlap — the mean adjacent-pixel difference in the
outer ring. **Designed**: `lede-winner` 0.8, `lede-collage` 1.9, `lede-lineup` 2.9.
**Photographed**: `lede-authority` 7.0, `lede-testing` 7.1, `lede-inuse` 10.0. Nothing sits
between 2.9 and 7.0 in 32 frames. Each prompt below carries its own family's clause and
nothing is imported from the product-page registry.

**Round 2 swaps the two designed forms** so the pair is tested on both types rather than
twice on one: round 1 gave `lede-winner` the gradient and `lede-collage` the flat tone; here
`lede-winner` takes a flat field and `lede-collage` takes the two-hue diagonal gradient.

**New products throughout.** None of the seven repeats a round-1 subject.

## 1 — `lede-pain` · lower back, seat cushion category · no product, no words

```
TYPE: lede-pain v0.2
REGISTER: editorial photojournalism, natural and unstaged. Single frame.

SUBJECT: a man in his fifties in a plain shirt, half-risen from a hard desk chair, one hand
flat on the small of his back and the other braced on the desk, weight still on the chair.

EVIDENCE: the stall in the movement is the physical fact — knees bent, hips not yet
straight, shoulders forward. He is not standing and not sitting.

COST: his coat is over the back of the chair and a colleague's empty desk is visible past
him, lights off. What the back is taking is the end of the day.

PLACE: an ordinary office at dusk, one window, the overhead lights off in his half of it.

GAZE: candid. He is not aware of the lens and does not look toward it.

GRADE: an ordinary photograph in ordinary light. Normal exposure, detail held in the
shadows and in the window, no filter and no colour cast.

GROUND: a real office behind him, mid-toned and almost colourless, thrown out of focus.
Unevenly lit — one side clearly darker. Not a sweep.

No product anywhere in the frame and none implied. No words, no numbers, no logo, no
badge. Nothing comes within a tenth of the width of any edge.
```

## 2 — `lede-inuse` · wall-mounted air cooler · one photo, no words

```
TYPE: lede-inuse v0.2
REGISTER: candid documentary photograph, single frame.

PRODUCT REFERENCE: the attached photo is the exact reference for the wall-mounted portable
air cooler. Preserve shape, proportions, material, finish and colour exactly.

SUBJECT: a woman in her thirties asleep on top of a made bed in a vest and shorts, one arm
over her eyes, in a room that is plainly hot — window open, curtain still.

ENVIRONMENT: her own bedroom in the late afternoon. A glass of water, a book face down, a
fan-shaped patch of light on the wall.

PRODUCT: the cooler mounted on the wall above the bed, running, present as the reason the
room is bearable rather than as the subject of the photograph.

GAZE: none. She is asleep and unaware of the lens.

LIGHT: even natural daylight, bright, soft shadows. No golden hour, no rim light.

GRADE: a natural palette, light grain, shallow depth of field. Honest, not glossy.

GROUND: a real bedroom wall, mid-toned and almost colourless, out of focus and unevenly
lit.

No words, no number, no display reading, no logo, no badge, no second person. Nothing comes
within a tenth of the width of any edge.
```

## 3 — `lede-lineup` · five external optical drives · FIVE photos, no words

```
TYPE: lede-lineup v0.4
REGISTER: editorial product photograph, one frame, no words in it.

PRODUCT REFERENCES: five attached photos, one per unit, in the order given. Each is the
exact reference for that unit — preserve shape, proportions, material, finish, colour and
every printed word on each exactly. The units are five different makers and their real
brand marks stay as the references show them.

FIELD: the five drives standing in a row on one surface, in the order of the attachments,
each turned so its disc slot and its ports read.

ARRANGEMENT: no unit favoured. The same height in frame, the same distance from the lens,
the same spacing, none centred and none forward of the others.

SURFACE: one pale oak worktop running the width of the frame, real and slightly worn,
taking a real contact shadow under each unit.

LIGHT: one broad soft source for the whole group, from the front and slightly above. No
unit separately lit.

GROUND: a smooth studio seamless behind the worktop, NEAR-WHITE — the drives are dark and
metallic, so the sweep stays out of their way. No wall, no room, no texture, no gradient,
no vignette, no visible join between the sweep and the worktop.

No rank number, no badge, no podium, no riser under any unit, no person, no word, no price
and no added logo. Nothing comes within a tenth of the width of any edge.
```

## 4 — `lede-testing` · cord and rope tightening tool · one photo, no legible reading

```
TYPE: lede-testing v0.3
REGISTER: editorial documentary photograph, one frame, no words in it.

PRODUCT REFERENCE: the attached photo is the exact reference for the cord and rope
tightening and cinching tool. Preserve shape, proportions, material, finish and colour
exactly.

UNIT: the tool clamped in a bench vice with a rope threaded through it, the rope running
taut out of frame to the right.

INSTRUMENT: a mechanical force gauge inline on the rope between the tool and the frame
edge, its hook engaged and the line under load.

HANDS: one hand on the vice handle and one steadying the rope, both cropped at the forearm.
The hands are working, not presenting.

SURFACE: a real bench — coiled rope, a spare carabiner, a marker pen. The tools of the
measurement and nothing decorative.

LIGHT: plain overhead working light. It falls off across the frame — one side of the
background is clearly darker than the other. No studio key, no rim, no fill.

GROUND: a REAL workshop behind the bench, mid-toned and almost colourless: racking, a
doorway, other work going on, all thrown out of focus. Not a sweep and not evenly lit.

The gauge's dial is turned away from the lens and no reading is legible anywhere. No chart,
no scale, no lab coat, no clipboard, no face, no word and no price. Nothing comes within a
tenth of the width of any edge.
```

## 5 — `lede-winner` · wireless translation earbuds · display type, `sticker` mark

```
TYPE: lede-winner v0.5
REGISTER: designed promotional composition, one frame.

PRODUCT REFERENCE: the attached photo is the exact reference for the wireless translation
earbuds and case. Preserve shape, proportions, material, finish and colour exactly.

SUBJECT: the open case with one earbud resting in it and the other standing beside it, CUT
OUT and floating — no surface under them and no shadow implying one. They hold the right
half of the picture.

GROUND: a designed field, light and strongly coloured, FLAT form — one single saturated
tone, a clear cobalt, filling the frame corner to corner. No gradient, no second hue, no
grain, no texture, no vignette, no scene.

DISPLAY TYPE: the single word EARBUDS in flat white capitals, set BEHIND the case so the
case overlaps it, and so large that the word runs off BOTH side edges — the E and the S are
cut by the frame. It is the largest thing in the picture after the product.

MARK: form `sticker`. Upper left, clear of the product, about a quarter of the picture
wide, tilted a few degrees as an applied label would be. A die-cut circle in a hot coral —
a colour neither the earbuds nor the ground carries — with a narrow white ring inset inside
its edge: BEST OVERALL in large white capitals over 2026 in small white capitals.

No score, star row, rating, certification seal, press logo or third-party award mark. The
display type may be cut by the side edges; the mark's words may not, and nothing else comes
within a tenth of the width of any edge.
```

## 6 — `lede-collage` · four hydraulic arm trainers · FOUR photos, gradient ground

```
TYPE: lede-collage v0.5
REGISTER: graphic product composition, one frame.

PRODUCT REFERENCES: four attached photos, one per unit, in the order given. Each is the
exact reference for that unit — preserve shape, proportions, material, finish and colour
exactly. The units are four different makers and their real brand marks stay as the
references show them.

CUT-OUTS: the four trainers, each cleanly cut out with no scene behind it, in the order of
the attachments.

ARRANGEMENT: a single row across the lower two thirds, read left to right, evenly spaced.
No unit favoured by size, height or position.

GROUND: a designed field, light and strongly coloured, GRADIENT form. A perfectly smooth
diagonal gradient from a clear lime at the upper left to a deep magenta at the lower right
— two hues opposite on the colour wheel. No grain, no texture, no vignette, no banding.

SHADOW: one faint contact shadow under each unit, identical for all four.

TITLE: across the empty upper band, two lines of flat white sans-serif, each beginning the
same distance from the left edge. The two lines fill that band margin to margin:
THE BEST FOUR
ARM TRAINERS

MARK: form `plaque`. Sitting OVER the second unit from the left, overlapping its upper
body, about a fifth of the picture wide. An upright rectangle in deep charcoal — a colour
neither the units nor the gradient carries — a thin white rule across it, TESTED in large
white capitals above and ALL FOUR in small white capitals below.

No rank number, no price, no podium, no scene, no person, no drop shadow under one unit
only, no certification seal, no press logo, no third-party award mark, no fabricated
rating. Nothing comes within a tenth of the width of any edge.
```

## 7 — `lede-authority` · anyone, unnamed · one photo, no words

Still the diagnostic of round 1, unchanged in purpose and on a new product: the type has no
skeleton in its own file, and this prompt does not create one. It strips everything that
makes the type illegal and asks what is left.

```
TYPE: lede-authority v0.1 — DIAGNOSTIC, not a skeleton
REGISTER: editorial documentary photograph, one frame, no words in it.

PRODUCT REFERENCE: the attached photo is the exact reference for the furniture lifting tool
set. Preserve shape, proportions, material, finish and colour exactly.

SUBJECT: a man in his forties in an ordinary sweatshirt, crouched beside a sofa in a
half-emptied living room, holding one lifter up at chest height and turned toward the lens,
mid-sentence as if explaining it to someone just out of frame.

ENVIRONMENT: a real room mid-move — boxes, a rolled rug, the sofa off its usual place.

LIGHT: plain daylight from a window to one side. No studio key, no rim, no seamless.

GRADE: a natural palette, light grain. Honest, not glossy.

GROUND: the room behind him, mid-toned and almost colourless, out of focus and unevenly
lit.

No name, no caption, no byline, no title card, no lab coat, no clipboard, no lanyard, no
certification seal, no star row, no logo, no word and no number anywhere in the picture.
Nothing comes within a tenth of the width of any edge.
```

## Grading

A verdict of `pass`, `partial` or `fail` per render — yours, under ADR-011. Then the
questions this round can settle:

| # | question | what it changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **In 3 and 6, did the renderer hold FIVE and FOUR real products from their own references?** | the whole multi-attachment route, and with it whether `lede-lineup` and `lede-collage` are makeable at all |
